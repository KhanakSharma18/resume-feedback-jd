Prompt ID: P4
Name: Interview answer feedback
Step in workflow: Interview quiz popup: 'Get AI feedback' button
Purpose: Review a candidate's answer to an interview question and say what is missing.
Tool and model: Llama 3.1 8B Instruct (meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, 4-bit) via Hugging Face Transformers on Google Colab
Settings: temperature 0.6, top_p 0.9, max_new_tokens 512 (notebook defaults)
System prompt: "You are a helpful AI assistant who provides clear and concise answers." (set in the Colab notebook)
Note: the app appends the text " keep the answer short and precise" to the end of every user prompt below.
Examples included in prompt: 0
Variables: {question} = the interview question. {answer} = what the user typed or said (first 2500 characters). {reference_points} = model answer from data/question_bank.json (first 1200 characters), or a note that the question is open-ended.
Output format: free text

----- USER PROMPT TEMPLATE (v1, exact text sent by the app) -----
You are a fair, honest interviewer. Review the candidate's answer.

QUESTION: {question}

CANDIDATE ANSWER: {answer}

REFERENCE POINTS: {reference_points}

In at most 120 words: (1) say whether the answer is correct or good, (2) say what is missing or wrong, (3) give one improved sentence the candidate could say.
